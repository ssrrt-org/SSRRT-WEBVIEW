About Lord Krishna

Cauvery Banks · Karekura Ashram Constituted 2018

Of all the divine incarnations in the vast tapestry of Hindu mythology, none has captured the human heart with quite the warmth, the beauty, and the multidimensional richness of Lord Krishna. He is at once the mischievous child stealing butter in Gokula, the enchantingly youthful cowherd whose flute calls forth the soul’s deepest longing, the loyal friend of Sudama, the beloved protector of Draupadi, and the supreme philosopher on the battlefield of Kurukshetra.

Krishna is among the most complete divine manifestations ever known to humanity — encompassing within his single form every quality that can be imagined in the Divine. Where other avatars emphasize righteous severity, Krishna introduces playful delight (Leela). Where austerity is demanded, Krishna offers unconditional love (Prema Bhakti).

In the Hindu understanding, Krishna is not merely a historical figure or a mythological character. He is a living presence — an aspect of the Supreme Consciousness that is eternally accessible to any sincere seeker. Situated on the serene banks of the sacred River Cauvery at Srimad Sai Rajarajeshwari Trust, the Vishnu Maya Temple stands as a consecrated portal where that intimate presence is felt in every temple bell, morning prayer, and gentle breeze from the goshala.

"मन्मना भव मद्भक्तो मद्याजी मां नमस्कुरु । मामेवैष्यसि सत्यं ते प्रतिजाने प्रियोऽसि मे ॥" — Bhagavad Gita 18.65: "Fix your mind on Me, be devoted to Me, sacrifice to Me, bow down to Me. In truth I promise you, for you are truly dear to Me."

✦ Spiritual Significance of the Krishna Temple at the Ashram

At Srimad Sai Rajarajeshwari Trust, the Vishnu Maya Krishna Temple is not an isolated stone shrine; it is the living emotional heartbeat of the entire sanctuary. Revered Amma has always emphasized the intimate, mother-child and companion relationship that Krishna invites from every devotee. Here, ritual is never mechanical. Devotees participate in Krishnacharana alongside the ashram’s sacred herd of indigenous cows, experiencing firsthand how divinity permeates natural, everyday rural life.

Situated where the Cauvery winds serenely past Mysore's fertile fields, the gentle river mist during Brahma Muhurta (4:30 AM) coats the temple bells. The temple sanctum is consecrated using traditional Agamic vidhis, invoking Vishnu Maya—the divine cosmic energy that both creates the wondrous variety of the world and lovingly removes the veil of illusion when surrendered unto Him.

BHAGAVAD GITA · CHAPTER 9, VERSE 26

पत्रं पुष्पं फलं तोयं यो मे भक्त्या प्रयच्छति ।
तदहं भक्त्युपहृतमश्नामि प्रयतात्मनः ॥

"Whoever offers Me with devotion a leaf, a flower, a fruit, or even water—that offering of devotion made by a pure-hearted seeker, I accept with immense delight."

✦ The Bhagavad Gita and the Teachings of the Krishna Temple

The philosophical cornerstone taught at Vishnu Maya Temple is Nishkama Karma—selfless action dedicated to the welfare of all beings without attachment to personal fruits. At SSRRT, every activity, from milking cows at 5:00 AM to chopping vegetables for community Narayana Seva and holding free medical clinics, is conducted as Krishna Arpanam (an offering to Krishna).

Pilgrims visiting the temple do not simply offer coconuts and depart; they are guided to seat themselves in the silent mandapa, absorb the Gita shlokas carved on the stone colonnades, and reflect on their personal life battles with renewed clarity, courage, and composure (*samattvam*).

✦ The Spiritual Advantages of Visiting the Krishna Temple

Darshan of Lord Krishna at Karekura creates an immediate dissolution of mental distress and anxiety. The combination of Cauvery’s purifying waters, the high spiritual vibrations maintained by uninterrupted chanting, and the pure sattvic atmosphere of 300+ lovingly sheltered cows creates a sanctuary where quiet introspection happens effortlessly. Devotees consistently recount experiences of sudden lightness in the heart, answers to deeply held spiritual quandaries, and a profound sense of protection under Govinda's grace.

Devotees of Bhakti

The temple mandapa remains uncrowded, silent, and contemplative throughout the afternoon. You are invited to sit undisturbed, chant the Hare Krishna Mahamantra or Vishnu Sahasranama, and soak in the sanctified stillness.

nature_people

After Gau Seva

Pilgrims completing Gau Pooja and feeding the cows find the walk directly into the Krishna sanctum to be a natural climax of their seva. In tradition, worshipping Govinda right after serving mother cow completes the sacred cycle of merit.

diversity_3

Families & Children

Children are welcomed with warmth. Feeding temple peacocks, observing the gentle calves in open pastures, and participating in the evening butter lamp lighting (Deepotsava) instills deep roots in Sanatana Dharma naturally.
