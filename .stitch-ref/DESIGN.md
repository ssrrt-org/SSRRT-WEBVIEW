## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a serene, venerable spiritual sanctuary situated on the banks of the sacred River Cauvery. The visual architecture blends quiet luxury with classical temple aesthetics: ivory parchment surfaces, deep sanctum indigos, warm ceremonial maroons, and refined gold filigree lines.

The emotional atmosphere is contemplative, eternal, and grounded in dignity (*dharma* and *shanti*). It deliberately eschews fleeting tech tropes—no hyper-saturated neons, aggressive hover states, or cold corporate grays. Instead, space is treated with architectural reverence, echoing carved stone mandapas, illuminated manuscripts, and the soft luminescence of temple oil lamps at dusk.

## Colors

The palette is anchored in natural earth pigments, mineral dyes, and traditional Indian temple sanctum tones:

- **Primary (`#222A45` - Deep Sacred Indigo):** The color of dusk over the Cauvery and sanctum shadows. Used for high-level headings, authority banners, navigation anchors, and rich dark section backdrops.
- **Secondary (`#9B3A1E` - Maroon Vermilion):** The ceremonial shade of sindoor and sacred temple silks. Reserved for primary calls to action, seva donations, spiritual registrations, and key interactive accents. Hover shifts deeper to `#7A2C15`.
- **Tertiary (`#C9973D` - Sanctum Gold):** Evoking brass lamps (*diyas*) and gilded temple spires. Used for eyebrow categorizations, sacred seals, delicate decorative dividers, and active states. On dark indigo surfaces, use `#E8C56A` for legibility; use `#DFC488` for architectural borders.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#FBF6EC` - Ivory Parchment):** The foundational backdrop, soft on the eyes and reminiscent of natural handmade paper. Tinted horizontal bands use `#F3EADA`, framed card containers use `#FFFFFF`, and muted structural strokes use `#E7DFCE`.
- **Body Neutral (`#2B2620` - Dark Charcoal):** A rich, organic off-black for long-form reading, supported by `#4A433A` for metadata, captions, and secondary notes.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy balances literary grace with high clarity:

- **Headlines:** Set in classical serif typography (`EB Garamond`), bringing timeless dignity to Ashram teachings, sacred titles, and architectural statements.
- **Body & UI Elements:** Set in `Work Sans`, providing clean, modern, and fatigue-free readability across event schedules, seva descriptions, and navigational menus.
- **Sacred Mantras & Sanskrit Verses:** Styled in italicized serif scales (`Noto Serif`), accompanied by generous line spacing to respect chanting rhythm and diacritical marks.
- **Eyebrow Headers & Sacred Labels:** Rendered in uppercase `Work Sans` with open tracking (`letter-spacing: 0.12em–0.14em`) and tinted with sanctum gold (`#C9973D`), acting as quiet signposts across sections.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon a centered, disciplined grid that prioritizes breathing space, symmetry, and contemplation:

- **Desktop (12 Columns):** Constrained to a maximum content container of 1240px, framed by generous margins (`4rem` / `64px`) to emulate manuscript margins. Gutters are held at `1.5rem` (`24px`).
- **Tablet (8 Columns):** Fluid layout with `2rem` (`32px`) margins and `1.25rem` (`20px`) gutters.
- **Mobile (4 Columns):** Compact single-column stacked hierarchy with `1.25rem` (`20px`) edge margins, ensuring scripture passages and forms maintain generous internal touch padding.
- **Vertical Rhythm:** Content sections transition through rhythmic breathing room (`space-xl` to `5rem`), punctuated by gold central dividers or lotus motifs rather than abrupt border separations.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through architectural stratification and tonal layering rather than heavy artificial drop shadows:

- **Tonal Layering:** The primary canvas rests on `#FBF6EC` (Ivory Parchment). Elevated cards and temple darshan containers lift slightly via pure white surfaces (`#FFFFFF`) framed by fine `#E7DFCE` hairline strokes.
- **Ambient Illumination:** Where elevation is necessary (such as modal dialogues, donation drawers, and darshan timings cards), shadows mimic warm candle/lamp falloff: `0 8px 30px rgba(34, 42, 69, 0.05), 0 1px 3px rgba(201, 151, 61, 0.08)`.
- **Gold Filigree Accents:** Deep sanctum indigo panels (`#222A45`) create immersive night darshan sections; elevation on these surfaces is indicated by double gold hairline borders (`#DFC488`, 1px solid) with inner inset offsets (4px padding) reminiscent of sacred mantapa plaques.

## Shapes

The design system employs a soft, architectural geometry (`roundedness: 1`):

- **Subtle Softness:** Base interactive elements (buttons, inputs, status badges) carry a slight `0.25rem` (4px) corner radius, avoiding cold sterility while rejecting overly playful pill forms.
- **Structural Integrity:** Large section containers and image frames utilize crisp `0.25rem` corners or strict right angles accented with decorative inner corner cuts or 1px sanctum gold frames.
- **Sacred Emblems:** Medallions, Trust seals, and guru portrait frames are strictly circular (`border-radius: 9999px`) accompanied by a subtle concentric gold border.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (Ceremonial CTA):** Background `#9B3A1E`, text `#FFFFFF`, border-radius `0.25rem`, subtle letter-spaced Work Sans 14px semi-bold. Hover transitions to `#7A2C15` with an ease of 250ms.
- **Secondary (Sanctum Outline):** Transparent background, 1px border `#222A45`, text `#222A45`. Hover transitions to background `#222A45`, text `#FBF6EC`.
- **Gold Accent (Devotional/Seva):** Background `#C9973D`, text `#222A45`, hover background `#B8852E`.
- **Ghost Button:** Text `#222A45` with gold underline accent (`#DFC488`) that expands outward on hover.

### Cards
- **Ashram & Darshan Cards:** Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) body, 1px structural border `#E7DFCE`, padded with `1.75rem`. Top border features an optional 3px decorative gold accent line (`#C9973D`).
- **Sacred Scripture Card:** Ivory parchment band (`#F3EADA`) background, centered serif verse, fine gold divider (`#DFC488`) separating Sanskrit chant from English translation.

### Chips & Eyebrow Badges
- Background `#F3EADA`, border 1px solid `#DFC488`, text `#9B3A1E` or `#C9973D`, font Work Sans 11px uppercase with `0.12em` letter spacing.

### Form Inputs
- Background `#FFFFFF`, border 1px solid `#E7DFCE`, placeholder text `#4A433A`, focus ring 1.5px solid `#222A45` with a subtle glow of `#C9973D` (10% opacity). Label in Work Sans 13px medium `#2B2620`.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Unchecked: 1.5px solid border `#E7DFCE`, background `#FFFFFF`.
- Checked: Background `#9B3A1E` with white tick; gold accent ring on focus.

### Dividers & Ritual Accents
- Horizontal separators are 1px solid `#DFC488`, frequently paired with a centered sacred diamond or lotus glyph in `#C9973D`.
