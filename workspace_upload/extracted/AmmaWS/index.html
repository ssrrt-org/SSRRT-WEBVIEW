Where seva becomes the quiet language of devotion

A living ashram community caring for cows, the rural poor, and consecrated spaces — offering medical aid, education, food, water, and daily worship to all who arrive at its gates.

Sanctuary for the sacred cow

Mother for Needy

Medical care, food, and presence for those who have little

Through camps, clinics, and daily distribution, the Trust carries medicine and meals to villages where both are scarce — treating every visitor as a guest of the Mother.

Medical Centers, Multi-Village

Permanent medical centers serving clusters of surrounding villages, offering general consultation, basic diagnostics, and medicines free of cost.

Find a medical center

Medical Support in the Village

Mobile health camps that travel directly into remote villages, bringing screening, treatment, and follow-up care to those who cannot travel.

Food for the Needy

Daily and weekly meal distribution ensuring that no person near the ashram, hospital, or village center goes without a wholesome meal.

Rural Upliftment

Building the foundations of a self-reliant village

From classrooms to clean water, the Trust's rural programs address the everyday needs that allow a community to grow with dignity.

Education

Tuition support, school supplies, and learning centers for children in villages with limited access to schooling.

Food Supplies

Monthly ration kits distributed to families identified through village outreach and local recommendation.

Water Supply

Borewells, hand pumps, and storage tanks installed in villages facing chronic water shortage.

Bedding Supplies

Blankets, mats, and bedding distributed ahead of winter to elderly residents and families in need.

Narayana Seva

Large-scale community meal offerings held on festival days and significant occasions, open to all.

Sevas

Offer a seva in your name or your family's

Sevas may be sponsored for a single day or on a recurring basis, and are performed with the devotion of the resident priests on the date you choose.

Nandi Abhisheka

A ceremonial bathing and adornment of the sacred bull Nandi, performed with milk, water, and fragrant offerings.

Butter Alankara for Ganesha

An elaborate decoration of Lord Ganesha's idol using fresh butter, shaped into traditional motifs by the temple sevaks.

Butter Alankara for Subramanya

A butter adornment offered to Lord Subramanya, a seva especially favored by devotees seeking protection and grace.
